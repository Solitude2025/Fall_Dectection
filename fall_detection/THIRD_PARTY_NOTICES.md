# Third-party components and data

This repository contains the project source code and the project's `checkpoints/best.pth` classifier checkpoint.

- Ultralytics YOLO pose weights (`yolov8n-pose.pt` and `yolo11m-pose.pt`) are **not** bundled. On first use, Ultralytics downloads them according to its own distribution terms. Review the current Ultralytics and model terms before commercial redistribution.
- OmniFall and any other videos used for training or evaluation are **not** bundled. Obtain them from their official source and follow the dataset's license, attribution, privacy, and redistribution rules.
- PyTorch, OpenCV, FastAPI, Spring Boot, MySQL, H2, and other dependencies remain under their respective licenses.
- Do not upload camera recordings, personally identifiable information, generated reports containing sensitive content, or production database files to this repository.
